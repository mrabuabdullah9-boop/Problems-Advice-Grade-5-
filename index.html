Talk in Pairs

Read the problem and the suggested advice.

1. Terrible toothache

A: What’s the matter?

B: I have a terrible toothache.

A: You should go to the dentist.

2. Bored at home

A: What’s the matter?

B: I am bored at home.

A: You should go for a walk.

3. Headache

A: What’s the matter?

B: I have a headache.

A: You should relax or get some sleep.

4. Earache

A: What’s the matter?

B: I have an earache.

A: You should go to the doctor.

5. Break my brother’s favorite toy

A: What’s the matter?

B: I broke my brother’s favorite toy.

A: You should buy him a new one.

6. Sore throat

A: What’s the matter?

B: I have a sore throat.

A: You should drink tea with honey.
